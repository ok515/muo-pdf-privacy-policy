MUO PDF — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Overview

MUO PDF is a PDF reader application. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

Information Collection

The app is intended to read PDF files locally on your device. The developer does not intentionally collect personal information through the app.

Files and Permissions

Files you choose to open are used to display their contents. File access depends on the permissions and file-selection features provided by your device.

Internet and Third Parties

This policy must be updated if the app uses internet access, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting tools, or third-party services.

Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes and Contact

This policy may be updated when the app changes. For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the app's official listing.

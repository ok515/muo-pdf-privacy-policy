MUO PDF — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

MUO PDF is an offline PDF reader designed to open and read PDF files on your device.

Data Collection

The app is designed not to collect, transmit, or share personal information.

Internet Access

The app is intended to work offline and does not require an internet connection for its PDF reading features.

Files and Permissions

PDF files are accessed locally on your device. The app does not upload PDF files to a server.

Third-Party Services

The app is not intended to use online advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children or other users.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the app's official store listing.
